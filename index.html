Aviamasters Playing Styles & Bankroll

There is no strategy that beats Aviamasters. The RTP is fixed and every round is random, so no betting pattern or 'system' changes your odds over time. What you can control is risk. Low-risk style: cash out early at small multipliers (around 1.2x-1.5x) for frequent, smaller wins. Balanced style: aim for mid-range multipliers and accept more swings. High-risk style: chase big multipliers, knowing most rounds will end first. Whichever you choose, set a budget and a loss limit before you start, keep each stake small relative to your bankroll, and stop when you hit your target or your limit. No 'hack', 'signal' or 'predictor' can guarantee a profit.
